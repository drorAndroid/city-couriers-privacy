City Couriers Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

City Couriers is made by Dror Zohar. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

What the game collects

We don't collect, receive or store any of your personal information. The game has no accounts, ads or analytics, and it doesn't send your progress anywhere.

What stays on your device

Your progress and settings, such as your stars, in-game cash, vehicles and options, are saved only on your device. They're deleted if you uninstall the game or choose Reset all progress. A record of your purchases is also kept there, so that none is paid out twice.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. Google processes your payment under its own terms and privacy policy, and we don't see your payment details. The game receives confirmation of what was bought, which it uses to deliver your purchase and doesn't send to us.

Internet use

The game only uses the internet to talk to Google Play for purchases. Everything else works offline.

Children

The game doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
