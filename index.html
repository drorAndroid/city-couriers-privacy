City Couriers Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

City Couriers is made by Dror Zohar. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

What the game collects

The game has no accounts of its own, ads or analytics, and we don't run a server that receives your information. Your progress isn't sent anywhere. The only exceptions are the optional Google services described below, which Google runs.

What stays on your device

Your progress and settings, such as your stars, in-game cash, vehicles, upgrades, race times and options, are saved only on your device. They're deleted if you uninstall the game or choose Reset all progress. A record of your purchases is also kept there, so that none is paid out twice.

Google Play Games leaderboards

The game can use Google Play Games for its weekly leaderboards. When you're signed in to Play Games, the game sends your best shift earnings and your best delivery streak to Google, linked to your Play Games player ID. Other players can see your score alongside your Play Games profile, such as your gamer name and avatar, depending on your Play Games privacy settings.

You can play the whole game without signing in, in which case no scores are sent. We can see and manage the leaderboards in Google's developer tools, including the scores and the Play Games names shown on them, but we don't get your email address or anything else from your Google Account. Google handles this data under its own terms and privacy policy. To change what others can see, or to manage or delete your game data, use your Play Games settings.

Photos

Pictures you take in photo mode are saved on your device. They only leave it if you choose to share one, and they go to the app you pick, under that app's own terms. We don't receive them.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. Google processes your payment under its own terms and privacy policy, and we don't see your payment details. The game receives confirmation of what was bought, which it uses to deliver your purchase and doesn't send to us.

Internet use

The game only uses the internet to talk to Google, for purchases, Play Games leaderboards and Google's optional prompt to rate the game. Everything else works offline. If you rate or review the game, Google handles that under its own terms, and we only see what Google Play publishes.

Children

The game isn't designed to collect personal information from children, and we don't knowingly do so. Whether a child can use Google Play Games, and what others can see, depends on Google's rules and the settings on their Google Account.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
